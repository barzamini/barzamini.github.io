I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Northern Illinois University, working under the supervision of Dr. Mona Rahimi. My research focuses on semantics-aware AI systems, requirements engineering for AI-enabled software, and evidence-based evaluation frameworks. I develop frameworks and tools for evaluating and improving AI-enabled perception systems, with a particular focus on safety-critical applications such as autonomous driving. My work combines requirements engineering, machine learning, and domain-driven design to create systematic approaches for dataset evaluation, model generalizability, and concept drift detection. My research addresses critical challenges in developing and evaluating AI-enabled software systems, particularly in safety-critical domains. I work on requirements engineering for AI systems, dataset quality assessment, black-box evaluation, concept drift detection, and safety-critical applications. I have developed frameworks achieving 95% F2-score for pedestrian detection and improved dataset alignment by 35% in autonomous driving systems.
