## Research Focus

My research addresses critical challenges in developing and evaluating AI-enabled software systems, particularly in safety-critical domains. I work on:

1. **Requirements Engineering for AI Systems**: Developing systematic approaches to specify, validate, and evaluate domain requirements in ML-based software using semantic web technologies and LLM-driven specifications.

2. **Dataset Quality Assessment**: Creating benchmarks and frameworks for evaluating dataset completeness, alignment with domain specifications, and identifying gaps that affect model performance.

3. **Black-Box Evaluation**: Designing automated evaluation frameworks that can assess AI perception systems without requiring access to model internals, using explainable AI techniques and domain semantics.

4. **Concept Drift Detection**: Building adaptive mechanisms to detect and handle domain shifts in AI systems, improving robustness against changing conditions.

5. **Safety-Critical Applications**: Applying these frameworks to autonomous driving and ADAS systems, ensuring higher fidelity in safety-critical scenarios.
